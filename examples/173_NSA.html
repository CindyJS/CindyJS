Non-standard analysis for degenerate constructions

Both widgets construct the sum x + y of the points X and Y (measured from O) on the line g, using joins and meets only. The construction needs an auxiliary point E off g. The result does not depend on E, but when E lies on g the construction degenerates: lines that should meet in a point coincide.

Drag E onto g (it snaps to the grid), along g, onto X or Y, and move X and Y while E stays on g. On the left the construction breaks down. On the right (nsa: true) undefined elements are evaluated infinitely close to the degenerate position with Levi-Civita numbers, and their limit is shown wherever it does not depend on how the position is approached. The sum is always such a limit, so it stays correct.
