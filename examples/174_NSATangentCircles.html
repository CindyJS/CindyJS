Non-standard analysis at a tangency

Two circles of radius 3 and the line r through their intersection points P and Q (the radical axis). Drag B, or use the buttons, so that the circles touch, and further apart, where P and Q become complex.

This widget uses nsa: true (see 173_NSA.html), but at a tangency there is nothing for it to do: P and Q coincide, yet their positions are defined, and so is r, the common tangent. Non-standard analysis only fills in values that would otherwise be undefined. It does not change which intersection is P and which is Q, and the tracing state still reports that the tangent position is singular. Moving on from there is handled by tracing, which continues from the last good position, just as without it.
