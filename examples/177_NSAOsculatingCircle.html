The osculating circle as a circle through three points

Both widgets construct, with ruler and compass, three points P1, P2, P3 on the parabola with focus F and directrix d (y = x²/4): Pi lies on the perpendicular to d at Xi, as far from F as from d. The circle k passes through the three points, Z is its center.

Move X2 onto X1: then P2 coincides with P1, and k, the circle through two points, is undefined. Its limit is the circle through P3 that touches the parabola at P1. Move X3 onto X1 as well: the limit of the circles through three points of a curve that run together is the osculating circle, the circle that fits the curve best at P1, whose radius is the radius of curvature, here 2(1 + x²/4)3/2 at x. On the left, k is undefined in both cases. On the right, with nsa: true (see 173_NSA.html), k is evaluated with infinitesimally separated points, and its limit, which does not depend on how they are separated, is shown: the radius agrees with the formula.

With "X1, X2, X3 together", drag them along d: Z, the center of curvature, runs along the evolute of the parabola (gray, Neile's parabola), and leaves a trail.
