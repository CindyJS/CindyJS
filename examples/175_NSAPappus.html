Pappus' theorem at degenerate positions

A, B, C lie on the line g and D, E, F on h, both through O. By Pappus' theorem the points X = AE ∩ BD, Y = AF ∩ CD and Z = BF ∩ CE are collinear; p (green) is the join of X and Z, and the text shows how far Y is off p (0 up to rounding).

Move points onto O or onto each other, by dragging or with the buttons (they move the points in both widgets): lines coincide, and some joins and meets are undefined. On the left they break down; on the right (nsa: true) they show their limit, approached along the last move, and Pappus' theorem still holds.
